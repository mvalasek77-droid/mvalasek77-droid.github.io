Cinema Composer — Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

Cinema Composer is a film-planning and editing tool. It is built so that we collect no data at all. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by us.

Data we collect

None. Your projects, budgets, timelines, bug reports and settings are stored locally on your device. We cannot read them and never receive them.

API keys you add yourself

Cinema Composer can call third-party AI services (video, voice, music and image tools) using your own API keys. Keys are stored only in your device's Keychain and are never exported or sent anywhere except the vendor you chose. When you run a live generation, the prompt and files for that generation travel directly from your device to that vendor under your key. That vendor's own privacy policy then applies to what they receive — we are not a party to it and never see the traffic.

Live runs are opt-in and off by default. Every built-in tool ships without an endpoint, so no live call is possible until you import a tool pack with real endpoints.

Subscriptions

Cinema Composer Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We receive no billing or personal information from the transaction.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes in a way that matters, the update ships with the app.
