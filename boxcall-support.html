Quick Fixes

  • Real posters not loading? Go to Profile → "Turn on real posters" and re-paste your free TMDB API key (tmdb.org → Settings → API). The key never leaves your device.
  • Trailer won't play? Trailers stream via YouTube; check your network connection or try again later.
  • Missing positions or streaks? Data is stored on-device; deleting and reinstalling the app resets your play-money portfolio.
  • Age gate rejected your year? BoxCall is for users 13+.

What BoxCall Is

BoxCall is a play-money social game — no real-money trading, no gambling, no purchases that affect odds. It is not a betting or financial app.
