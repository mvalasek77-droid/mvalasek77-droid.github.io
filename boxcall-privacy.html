BoxCall — Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

BoxCall is a social prediction game for movie opening-weekend box office. All trading uses play money — no real money is ever at stake. We collect no personal data: there is no account signup, no analytics, no advertising, and no server operated by us that receives your data.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, emails, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or any identifier that links to your real identity. The app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your trading profile (handle, XP, tier, followers, badges, portfolio, positions) is generated inside the app and stored only on your device. Your handle and posts exist within the app's demo community feed; they are not transmitted to any server we operate.

Optional Third-Party Content

BoxCall can display real movie information. The built-in catalog is bundled with the app. If you turn on real posters, the app fetches poster images from The Movie Database (TMDB) using an API key you provide and that stays on your device. Those requests go directly from your device to TMDB, subject to TMDB's own privacy terms. Trailers play via YouTube's standard embedded player; YouTube may apply its own privacy policy when streaming. We never see this traffic.

Children

The app is intended for users 13 and older. It includes an age gate on first launch and contains no content designed for young children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will replace this page.
