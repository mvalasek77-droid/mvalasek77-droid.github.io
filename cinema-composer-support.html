Cinema Composer — Support

Getting started

Everything in Cinema Composer works offline with no account. Start on the Get Started tab: import or write a script, and the breakdown, budget and schedule build themselves.

Common questions

Do I need API keys? No. Every planning and budgeting feature works with no keys at all. The Conductor's dry run simulates a full production and bills nothing.

How do I try the paid tier free? Tap the locked Cutting Room tab and choose "Or try the demo cut" — the full editor opens on a showcase sequence.

Where are my keys stored? In your device's Keychain. They are never exported or sent anywhere except the vendor you chose.
